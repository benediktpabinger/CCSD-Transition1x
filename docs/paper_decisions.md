# Decision log for the RQ2 paper (OMol25 models at broken-symmetry transition states)

One entry per decision, newest at the bottom. Each entry says what was decided, what the
alternatives were, why, and which file or job the evidence is in. Open points are collected
at the end. Keep this file current: every decision that will have to be justified in the
paper's Methods section belongs here, with the date it was taken.

Vocabulary as in the thesis: closed-shell / broken-symmetry (groups), stable / unstable
(restricted solution), restricted / unrestricted surface, "level of theory" = functional and
basis only, "OMol25 protocol" = level + unrestricted + route to the broken-symmetry solution.

## Open points (not yet decided)

- **The <S^2> threshold of 0.05 has no gap to sit in at dataset scale.** Among the 10 009 OMol25 labels, 7 557 are exactly 0, 1 119 below 1e-4, 126 between 1e-3 and 0.05, and 87 between 0.05 and 0.1 (`results/omol25_ts_all.csv`). The thesis saw nothing between 0 and 0.058 in its 135 structures. **Border-zone check done (D13):** the zone is a continuum of shallow breakings, 0.05 in <S^2> corresponds to about 1 meV in depth and separates nothing physical. Proposal: define the groups for the paper by **breaking depth** (e.g. closed-shell: depth < 1 meV or stable; broken-symmetry: depth > 10 meV; the 1-10 meV band either excluded or reported as its own stratum), with <S^2> kept as the descriptive quantity. This needs the depth at every TS in the pool, i.e. the RKS single point next to the UKS one, which the thesis protocol already runs for every structure. Decide before the pilot.
- **OMol25's rotation misses deep BS solutions in about 3 % of the border zone** (7 of 213, 0.16-0.46 eV below the label, `results/border_zone.csv`), and the thesis saw 1 of 135 at the model TS. **Sample of the <S^2> <= 0.001 labels done (2026-10-29, `results/zero_sample.csv`): 0 of 200 unstable**, all lambda_min > 0, so the miss rate among the "closed-shell" labels is below about 1.5 % (95 %). The misses sit where the rotation found something but too little, not where it found nothing. Consequences: (a) the pre-selection by OMol25 <S^2> (D04) is sound for the closed-shell side; the broken-symmetry side gains the 7 (and any further ones among the 87 with 0.05 < s2 <= 0.1 are already in the border-zone table); (b) still a result for the paper: OMol25 labels on the wrong SCF solution at transition states, by hundreds of meV, for roughly 7 of the 1 239 reactions with any symmetry breaking.
- **N_FOD at all 10 073 stored TS done (2026-10-29, `results/fod_ts_all.csv`):** broken-symmetry median 0.67, closed-shell 0.16, rho = 0.61; 1 691 closed-shell reactions lie inside the 10-90 % N_FOD window of the broken-symmetry ones, so matched pairs (D05) exist for the whole pool.
- **ORCA on the cluster.** No el9 tree has ORCA (checked 2026-10-27), and the admins will not have it there for months (OpenMPI incompatibility). All ORCA jobs run on `xeon24el8` / `xeon40el8` with `ORCA/5.0.4-gompi-2023a` (D15) while those nodes last; the admins keep them for ORCA users for now. Risk: if el8 is retired before the DFT is done, the remaining calculations wait for the older MPI on el9. Keep the DFT steps early in the schedule.
- **el8 capacity is shrinking and fully booked.** 2026-10-28: xeon24el8 62 nodes accepting jobs (72 before), xeon40el8 56 (99 before), all allocated, 279 and 989 jobs pending in front of ours, 10 and 43 nodes marked for reinstall. The queue, not the compute, sets the schedule for every ORCA step; the 1500 node-hours of the full run would take weeks here. To raise with Arghya (he is the largest el8 user right now): group fair-share, how long he needs el8, whether the DFT steps can be queued early.
- **Preprint first?** Proposed 2026-10-28: a short preprint (ChemRxiv or arXiv physics.chem-ph) with the 45-reaction results plus the dataset-wide census numbers (11.5 % BS at the stored TS, residual force 1.07 vs 0.37 eV/A over 10 009 reactions), the mechanism left open; the scaled-up study as the full paper later. Reasons: result is finished, timestamp against competing groups, ORCA/el8 risk makes the full run's date uncertain. Needs Arghya's decision on venue and authors.
- **Pool size and matching rule.** Whether to take all ~1150 broken-symmetry reactions or a subset, and how the closed-shell partner is chosen (nearest N_FOD, same formula, or both). Depends on the N_FOD scan of all stored transition states, which has not run yet.
- **Pilot.** Planned: about 200 reactions through the reworked pipeline first, to measure NEB and SCF failure rates and real timings before the full run.
- **Models.** UMA-S, UMA-M and eSEN as in the thesis; MACE-OMol25 is the strongest model in Marks et al. and was listed as missing in the thesis limitations. Not decided whether to add it.
- **Consistently trained control.** Running the Chapter 3 MACE model (restricted labels at restricted geometries) on the same reactions and checking its transition states with restricted DFT would test the training-data hypothesis at low cost. Not decided.
- **Unrestricted reference saddles.** Whether to attempt real broken-symmetry saddle optimisations (`bs_tsopt_v2.py`, `bs_uks_neb.py`) for a subset, so that the paper can report distance to the true saddle instead of only "not stationary".
- **Statistics.** Report cluster-bootstrap intervals over reactions rather than structures (135 structures of 45 reactions are not independent). Resolve the breaking-depth wording of the thesis (rho = -0.05 "independent of depth" vs. "falls to the closed-shell level above 1.3 eV").
- **Pipeline rework.** Array jobs bundling many NEBs or single points per node, automatic resubmission of failed SCFs, statistical checks instead of frozen exception lists by name.
- **Compute allocation.** About 35k core-hours on Niflheim: check fair-share with Arghya or the cluster admins before the full run.
- **Target venue and format.** Workshop paper (current data plus statistics fixes) vs. full paper (JCTC, JCP, Digital Discovery) needing the scale-up.
