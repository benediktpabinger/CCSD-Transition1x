# Decision log for the RQ2 paper (OMol25 models at broken-symmetry transition states)

One entry per decision, newest at the bottom. Each entry says what was decided, what the
alternatives were, why, and which file or job the evidence is in. Open points are collected
at the end. Keep this file current: every decision that will have to be justified in the
paper's Methods section belongs here, with the date it was taken.

Vocabulary as in the thesis: closed-shell / broken-symmetry (groups), stable / unstable
(restricted solution), restricted / unrestricted surface, "level of theory" = functional and
basis only, "OMol25 protocol" = level + unrestricted + route to the broken-symmetry solution.

## Open points (not yet decided)

- **The <S^2> threshold of 0.05 has no gap to sit in at dataset scale.** Among the 10 009 OMol25 labels, 7 557 are exactly 0, 1 119 below 1e-4, 126 between 1e-3 and 0.05, and 87 between 0.05 and 0.1 (`results/omol25_ts_all.csv`). The thesis saw nothing between 0 and 0.058 in its 135 structures. Options: keep 0.05 (thesis continuity) and treat 0.01-0.1 as a border zone that the final stability analysis (D06) settles; or exclude the border zone from the pool. Decide before the pilot.
- **Pool size and matching rule.** Whether to take all ~1150 broken-symmetry reactions or a subset, and how the closed-shell partner is chosen (nearest N_FOD, same formula, or both). Depends on the N_FOD scan of all stored transition states, which has not run yet.
- **Pilot.** Planned: about 200 reactions through the reworked pipeline first, to measure NEB and SCF failure rates and real timings before the full run.
- **Models.** UMA-S, UMA-M and eSEN as in the thesis; MACE-OMol25 is the strongest model in Marks et al. and was listed as missing in the thesis limitations. Not decided whether to add it.
- **Consistently trained control.** Running the Chapter 3 MACE model (restricted labels at restricted geometries) on the same reactions and checking its transition states with restricted DFT would test the training-data hypothesis at low cost. Not decided.
- **Unrestricted reference saddles.** Whether to attempt real broken-symmetry saddle optimisations (`bs_tsopt_v2.py`, `bs_uks_neb.py`) for a subset, so that the paper can report distance to the true saddle instead of only "not stationary".
- **Statistics.** Report cluster-bootstrap intervals over reactions rather than structures (135 structures of 45 reactions are not independent). Resolve the breaking-depth wording of the thesis (rho = -0.05 "independent of depth" vs. "falls to the closed-shell level above 1.3 eV").
- **Pipeline rework.** Array jobs bundling many NEBs or single points per node, automatic resubmission of failed SCFs, statistical checks instead of frozen exception lists by name.
- **Compute allocation.** About 35k core-hours on Niflheim: check fair-share with Arghya or the cluster admins before the full run.
- **Target venue and format.** Workshop paper (current data plus statistics fixes) vs. full paper (JCTC, JCP, Digital Discovery) needing the scale-up.
