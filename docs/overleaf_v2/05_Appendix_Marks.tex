\chapter{Stability of the Marks et al.\ reference transition states}
\label{app:marks}

Marks et al.~\cite{marks2026} do not report the SCF solution at their
reference saddles. We ran the stability analysis ourselves, at the
paper's level, \mbox{$\omega$B97X-V/def2-TZVP}, so that the result
describes the surface the references were optimised on.

The geometries come from the FSM repository of the Gomes
group\footnote{\texttt{github.com/thegomeslab/fsm}, released with
Marks and Gomes~\cite{marks2024fsm} at the same level of theory.}. Five Sharada
references coincide with Baker references, so the set holds 28
distinct structures\footnote{Marks et al.\ count 29 reactions
($24 + 5$), that is, four shared references. In the published
geometries five Sharada references coincide with Baker ones, three
exactly and two to within 0.0015~\AA, and their rows in the paper's
tables are identical; we therefore count 28.}, three of them
open-shell. The 25 closed-shell
singlets were tested with the protocol of Section~\ref{sec:dft} in
ORCA 5.0.4: a restricted single point with gradient, which confirms
that every geometry is a stationary point at this level, then an
unrestricted single point with stability analysis and restart where
the restricted solution is unstable.

24 of the 25 are stable (Table~\ref{tab:marks-stability}), none marginally: the smallest stability
eigenvalue is $+0.021$~au, an order of magnitude above the marginal
cases of the Transition1x set. The one unstable reference is the
transition state of bicyclo[1.1.0]butane to trans-butadiene, with
$\langle S^2\rangle = 0.19$ and a breaking depth of 13.7~meV; Hait et
al.~\cite{hait2025} found the same structure unstable at
B3LYP/def2-SVP. It is still the restricted saddle, and the models
reproduce it: under CI-NEB all four reach it in three to four gradient
calls. The reactions they fail on most often are stable ones.

With one unstable reference among 25, this benchmark cannot show
whether instability affects transition-state search. That the Baker
and Sharada reactions are almost uniformly stable is the finding; the
question needs a set selected for multireference character, which is
what Chapter~\ref{ch:mr} uses.
