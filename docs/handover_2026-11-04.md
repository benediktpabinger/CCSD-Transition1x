## State of the paper

Core claim (holds up): force error of the four OMol25 models (UMA-S, UMA-M, eSEN, MACE-OMol)
rises about 1.5-2x with multireference character (N_FOD), at equal bond stretch and size, on
stored transition states (all four models), at found structures (eSEN, UMA-M) and off the
training path (eSEN). Broken symmetry adds little at equal MR (1.0-1.3x). Barriers at the
found structures stay mostly within chemical accuracy.

Search result: freezing string + Sella, valid transition state on the model surface, about
86-87 % for low-MR baseline, 48-53 % twins, 32-39 % BS, 17-23 % for N_FOD >= 1 (all four
models, `results/search_checks.csv`). Interpolated-start NEB shows a strong BS penalty
(paired p <= 0.001). Every reaction with N_FOD >= 1 in the sample is BS, so MR and BS cannot
be separated there.

Not usable yet: the DFT refinement pilot (D32, `~/marks_pilot`). See audit items below.
