been much less fun and much less successful without you.

Thank you, Andreas, for introducing me to this field in the first place, and
even more for all the time you put into discussing, brainstorming and
helping me in every way. The days together in Vienna pushed this thesis
towards its most interesting part. And even from Toronto, you were always
just one useful Slack message away.
