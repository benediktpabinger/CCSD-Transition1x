\subsection{Label Check}
\label{sec:labelcheck}
We recomputed 2{,}490 Transition1x transition states from all four groups with the protocol of Section~2.1. For 2{,}452 of them, the energy agrees with the OMol25 label to within 1\,meV. For the remaining 38, the stability analysis finds a lower solution than the one in OMol25, and 24 of these change from closed-shell to broken-symmetry. Our energy is never above the OMol25 label. The OMol25 labels are therefore reliable for most of these structures, but the guess rotation occasionally misses a lower broken-symmetry solution.

\subsection{Force Errors}
\label{sec:forces}
Figure~\ref{fgr:forces} shows the force error of each model at its located transition states against $N_\mathrm{FOD}$ at the same structure. For closed-shell transition states with low multireference character, the median force error is 6--13\,meV/\AA{}. It rises with $N_\mathrm{FOD}$ and is largest around $N_\mathrm{FOD} = 1$--1.2. The few structures above $N_\mathrm{FOD} = 1.5$ have lower errors again, although mostly still above those at low $N_\mathrm{FOD}$. At $N_\mathrm{FOD} \geq 1$, the median force error is 21--54\,meV/\AA{}, three to four times the value at low multireference character for all four models (Table~\ref{tbl:ratios}).

At similar multireference character ($0.4 \leq N_\mathrm{FOD} < 0.9$), broken-symmetry transition states have larger force errors than closed-shell ones for all four models, by a factor of 1.2 to 1.6 (Table~\ref{tbl:ratios}).
