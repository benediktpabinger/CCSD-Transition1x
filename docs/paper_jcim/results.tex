\subsection{Evaluation at the Transition1x Transition States}
\label{sec:labelcheck}
We recomputed 2{,}490 Transition1x transition states from all four groups with the protocol of Section~2.1. For 2{,}452 of them, the energy agrees with the OMol25 label to within 1\,meV. For the remaining 38, the stability analysis finds a lower solution than the one in OMol25, and 24 of these change from closed-shell to broken-symmetry. Our energy is never above the OMol25 label. The OMol25 labels are therefore reliable for most of these structures, but the guess rotation occasionally misses a lower broken-symmetry solution.

% Numbers: results/training_point_summary.csv (pipeline/training_point_summary.py), all 10,009 TS
At the same structures, we evaluate the models against the OMol25 labels. For closed-shell transition states with low multireference character, the median force error against the OMol25 label is 5--12\,meV/\AA{}. At $N_\mathrm{FOD} \geq 1$, it is 11--25\,meV/\AA{}, 1.9 to 2.3 times the value at low multireference character for all four models (95\,\% confidence intervals 1.7--2.7). The force error therefore rises with multireference character even at structures the models were trained on. MACE-T1x, trained only on restricted $\omega$B97X/6-31G(d) labels, shows the same rise against its own labels at the transition states of its training split: 17.5 to 40.7\,meV/\AA{}, a factor of 2.3 (95\,\% confidence interval 2.1--2.5).

\subsection{Force Errors at the Located Transition States}
\label{sec:forces}
Figure~\ref{fgr:forces} shows the force error of each model at its located transition states against $N_\mathrm{FOD}$ at the same structure. For closed-shell transition states with low multireference character, the median force error is 6--13\,meV/\AA{}. It rises with $N_\mathrm{FOD}$ and is largest around $N_\mathrm{FOD} = 1$--1.2. The few structures above $N_\mathrm{FOD} = 1.5$ have lower errors again, although mostly still above those at low $N_\mathrm{FOD}$. At $N_\mathrm{FOD} \geq 1$, the median force error is 21--54\,meV/\AA{}, three to four times the value at low multireference character for all four models (Table~\ref{tbl:ratios}), compared with about two times at the Transition1x transition states (Section~\ref{sec:labelcheck}). UMA-M has the smallest force errors at both low and high multireference character, and MACE-OMol the largest at high multireference character, but the relative increase is similar for all models.

Broken-symmetry transition states usually have higher $N_\mathrm{FOD}$ than closed-shell ones. To separate the effect of the spin class from that of $N_\mathrm{FOD}$, we compare the two classes in the range $0.4 \leq N_\mathrm{FOD} < 0.9$, where both occur with nearly the same median $N_\mathrm{FOD}$ (Table~\ref{tbl:ratios}). In this range, broken-symmetry transition states still have larger force errors: their median is 1.2 to 1.6 times that of closed-shell ones, depending on the model. A regression on $N_\mathrm{FOD}$ and spin class and pairs matched in $N_\mathrm{FOD}$ give similar factors (SI). The larger force errors at broken-symmetry transition states therefore do not follow from their higher $N_\mathrm{FOD}$ alone.

\subsection{Barrier Errors at the Located Transition States}
\label{sec:barriers}
Figure~\ref{fgr:barriers} shows the absolute barrier error of each model at its located transition states against $N_\mathrm{FOD}$ at the same structure. For closed-shell transition states with low multireference character, the median barrier error is 2--5\,meV, well below chemical accuracy (43\,meV). It rises with $N_\mathrm{FOD}$ and is largest around $N_\mathrm{FOD} = 1$--1.5. The few structures above $N_\mathrm{FOD} = 1.6$ have lower errors again. At $N_\mathrm{FOD} \geq 1$, the median barrier error is 10--31\,meV, three to eight times the value at low multireference character (Table~\ref{tbl:ratios}). Most barriers therefore remain within chemical accuracy, but the share that does not grows from 0--2\,\% at low multireference character to 11\,\% (UMA-M), 21\,\% (UMA-S), 27\,\% (eSEN) and 38\,\% (MACE-OMol) at $N_\mathrm{FOD} \geq 1$. UMA-M has the smallest barrier errors at high multireference character and the smallest increase, and MACE-OMol the largest barrier errors at both low and high multireference character.

In the range $0.4 \leq N_\mathrm{FOD} < 0.9$, where both spin classes occur with nearly the same median $N_\mathrm{FOD}$ (Table~\ref{tbl:ratios}), broken-symmetry transition states have larger barrier errors for eSEN and UMA-S: their median is 2.4 and 2.3 times that of closed-shell ones. For UMA-M and MACE-OMol, the difference is not clear, and the confidence intervals include 1. For barriers, the effect of the spin class beyond $N_\mathrm{FOD}$ therefore depends on the model.
