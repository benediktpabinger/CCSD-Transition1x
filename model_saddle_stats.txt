============================================================================
1b  THE SAME QUESTION FOR OUR OWN STRUCTURES AND FOR THE BENCHMARK
    REFERENCE
============================================================================
The reference is the ORCA NEB transition state the whole benchmark is
scored against. Its gradient is measured on the ground-state surface,
which for the multireference reactions is the broken-symmetry one -- so
the number says whether the reference is a stationary point of the
surface the reaction actually runs on. No Hessian was ever computed at
the reference, so its stage 2 is unknown by construction.

                         n  stationary   1 imag  saddle   share
ours, multireference    16          16       16      16    100%
reference, multireference  19           0  unknown unknown           median grad 1.697 eV/A
reference, single-reference  26          25  unknown unknown           median grad 0.043 eV/A

Our structures are compared on their own terms: they were optimised to
be saddles, so a high rate here is expected and is a check on the
optimisation, not a result about the models. The reference row is the
one that carries information -- it is what every RMSD in the benchmark
is measured against.
